Everything that decides your GPA, in one place.

Open it between classes and know where you stand in five seconds. Sit down with it before exams and plan exactly what you need.

  • Transcript import

    Upload the PDF Mosaic gives you. Every past course and grade lands in your record, with your GPA on both the 4.0 and 12.0 scales.

  • Syllabus autofill

    Drop in a course outline and the assessment table fills itself: every quiz, midterm and final with its weight, across each marking scheme.

  • What you need

    Enter the grades you have so far and see the mark you need on what is left to finish the course where you want it.

  • Due dates, synced

    Every assessment date goes on one calendar. Send it to Google Calendar so deadlines show up next to the rest of your week.

  • Private by default

    Your grades are encrypted and stay yours. The leaderboard is opt-in and never shows a grade, only a handle, an avatar and Track Points.

A leaderboard you win by improving.

You are ranked on progress toward a target you set, not on raw GPA. A first-year climbing hard can sit above a fourth-year coasting.

  • Your baseline, your target

    Your transcript sets where you start. You pick where you are aiming, and both freeze for the season once you join.

  • Aim higher, score higher

    Aiming a full point or more above your baseline plays Growth. Anything less plays Maintenance.

  • Leave whenever you like

    Nothing is recorded against you, and your grades never appear on the board.
